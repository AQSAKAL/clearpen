Writers revise more when they reread, and they reread when they pause. Tools that suggest the next sentence remove the pause: the suggestion arrives before the writer has reread what they wrote. The pause can be restored by holding any suggestion back for two seconds, and we asked whether that changes what writers revise and how much.

## Method

Forty writers took part, 20 in each condition. Each wrote a [length] piece on a topic of their choice in an editor that suggested the next sentence, under two conditions: a two-second hold before any suggestion, which we call the pause condition, and no hold, which we call the flow condition. The hold itself was settled after many attempts: two seconds is the shortest that writers noticed.

We measured the RTR for each writer, together with the number of revisions that changed a claim rather than a word.
